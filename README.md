# Will

> **An intelligent AI platform built to help you think, create, automate, and get things done.**

Will is an intelligent AI platform developed by **Oghosa**, designed to bring conversation, reasoning, creativity, automation, and intelligent execution into one system.

Will goes beyond answering questions. It is designed to understand requests, reason through problems, use tools, interact with connected systems, create content, and take action across the digital and physical world.

From everyday conversations and complex reasoning to smart-space automation, generative media, AI-powered commerce, and multi-asset trading, Will is being built to become an intelligent layer between people and the technology they use.
